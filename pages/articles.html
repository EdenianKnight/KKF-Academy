🎾 Why Are Tennis Balls Yellow?

Your child might know. Your friends might know. Do you?

We bet you didn't know that Sir David Attenborough played a part in the story behind tennis balls switching from white to optic yellow. Here's your chance to learn something phenomenal, discover the fascinating Physics behind it, and pick up a little knowledge you can casually drop into conversation later. Because at some point, you'll be sitting around with family or friends when the opportunity presents itself. Just casually say: “You know tennis balls weren't always yellow, right?” Now stop. Smile. Whatever they say next, enjoy your five minutes of fame. And when someone inevitably asks: “How do you know that?” Just smile again and say: “Google it.”
